What the fuck is going on?

It’s somewhat not even a question anymore. It has become a state of mind and I’m pretty sure we all feel this nowadays with all the shits happening around us. As soon as I got out of school, I have started to feel this way. Like: shit, you have to do something with your life now, you have to be “somebody”. But I think that was more of an internal struggle though. Slowly and steadily, I realized that how pretty much everyone who doesn’t live under a rock (or have rich parents) also think, but in a more collecttively, boarder sense. Like: “Huh, it a whole big mess, I didn’t start it, I was born IN it.”  And now, I think everyone, or mostly people my age, all feel the same and we joyfully just suffer, swim in this pile of WHATEVER THE FUCK IS GOING ON together.

Isn’t that fucked up?

I saw this awhile ago from a David Chapelle’s comedy special:

“...But you at your age don’t even know. Because in your generation, it’s like the space shuttle blows up every fucking day. How can you care about anything, when know goddamn everything. I’m getting over on cop shooting and then another one happens… and then another one happens. I’m crying about Paris and then Brussels happens. I can’t keep track of all this shits so you just give the fuck up…”

“...This is the age of spin. The age where nobody knows what the fuck they’re even looking at”.

Even though I don’t think the special was really good, those words just stay with me.

Everyday we open our phones, going through social media, news and whatever the hell we are looking at nowadays. We either see brainrot dumbass shits or like the second, third 9/11 of the year. But somehow, we have become so numb, emotionally unattached to the bad events all around us. Maybe I am just making a big assumption here, at least it is the way I feel. And how else could anyone go through the day now? Is it a survival method for our mental or what?

“Ignorance is bliss” - I fucking hate this saying, since it has been the main cause of most of my personal problems. But I still think is true in a sad way. It doesn’t solve anything, but it sure helps.

I don’t even think the worst thing about this day and age is all these terrible events around the world.

It is how they make us forget our own battle inside.

Something parents of our generations always say: “The kids somewhere else have a much worse lives than your.”Firstly, how the hell does that help me? Secondly, how the fuck does that help “the kids somewhere else”? In general, how the fuck does that help anyone? But ironically, I (I’m not going to say “we” anymore since I think this is the particular way i feel.) can’t help myself thinking all the time:

”Yeah, my life isn’t that bad. There are worse things happen all around the world, people who were dealt a worse hand in life. I’m just exaggerating.”

Again, this thought has never solved any problem in my life. From my “spoiled” point of view, life itself is already pretty too much to handle. No matter your origin, your situation, it is already HARD. Maybe your diamond ring is too heavy or your Lamborghini is too yellow? But never before in the history of humanity have we ever had to “deal” with, process other problems. Fortunately most of us are not psychopath, we still empathize with the things we see, listen and other people. In one hand, I think it is an amazing ability that we share, on the other hand, it’s just fucking too much to handle for each of us individually. This world is bigger than all of us, so its problems also appear bigger than ours. I don’t think that should be how we see it and I’m trying to change it in how I see things now.

Of course, politically, finacially, culturally… whatever, they have an undeniable effect on us all. But in a egoistic way, I think the best solution for us all is to put ourselves first, to create a seperated “frame of minds”, where all these problems, battles dont collide. I don’t know how exactly, ask Buddha. (And please don’t tell me about all these life coaching books or personalities because I’m going to tell you why they are all bullshits). I think i’m getting closer though. We all do, as the people before have also survived, existed and lived. It is just the matter of time. And time always runs out, it keeps running out. Take a listen, Time in a bottle - Jim Croce and you’ll know what I mean with time.

That’s why I have quitted social medias for awhile now. Both of my worlds don’t look so pretty right now. And honestly I stopped thinking “what the fuck is going on?” a long time ago. Now, all I can think of is:

“What the fuck am I gonna do…”
