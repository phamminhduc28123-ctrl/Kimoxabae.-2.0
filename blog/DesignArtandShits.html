Design, Art and Shits

If I was going to define myself with one trait, the last word I would choose is ”Artist”. Not that I hate art or creating it, as the matter of fact, it is the one thing I can’t live without. I have spent enough time among artists and  the art world, one thing I can say for sure is that it is so fake and pretentious.

At this point, you probably think this is going to be a hate rant, so let me tell you how much I love to create, how much I appreciate it that it pains me to see how people keep destroying it.

To me, art is not an ability, a hobby, a right…

it was privilege, then freedom and now, my life.

You would think a kid drawing in his old notebooks or a piece of paper something happens all around the world, all the time. Well that was pretty much my childhood too, the difference was that was all I did as a kid and I was very bad at school. Despite my incompetence at school and even later in pretty much everything, my parents somehow thought: “He doesn’t seem good at this, let him do more of it.”

So art and creativity or “recreational” activities were pretty much replaced with extra classes after school at night. Physic, Math, English… and whatever my mom heard from her colleagues at work or gossips from other mom. Didn’t matter how much she heard. If another kid is doing something extra, special academically? I have to do it too. Who cares if I didn’t have time to explore my capabilities, passion and possibly my future career and even dream job?
